# Feedback Tracker

Every piece of stakeholder feedback, typed. Routed here from the `💬 Feedback Log` section of the Granola template.

| Date | From | Feedback | Type | Response | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Name (role) | What they actually said | ✅ Valid | Who responded and how | Open |

## Types

| Type | Meaning | How to handle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ✅ Valid concern | A real problem with the design | Acknowledge, fix, record the fix as a decision |
| ❓ Misunderstanding | They read the design wrong | Re-frame on the spot, cite the rationale. Do not change the design |
| 👍 Personal preference | Taste, not a problem | Acknowledge the preference, restate why the current choice stands |

## Rules

- **Record who responded**, not just what was said. Do not assume the designer answered.
- **A `👍 Personal preference` never becomes a design change on its own.** If it does change the design, something else justified it — record that instead.
- **Unaddressed ✅ Valid concerns are the best predictor of your next design review.** Scan this file before every sync.
